Meet the Builder | Forrest Stout

Forrest started Stout Builders with a simple belief — he could make an impact through his God-given gifts and talents to bless many lives within his community and this industry. Driven by that mission and a keen eye for unique home design and functionality, he set out to build a company where people come before profit and integrity rules over everything. He believes that a home is more than the sticks and bricks, but rather the lives of those who will live in it for years to come. From concept to completion, Forrest is hands on and committed to excellence. He cares, and it shows. Investing in people and improving the industry, one home at a time.
