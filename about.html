Forrest Stout

Forrest started Stout Builders with a simple belief — that he could make an impact through his God-given gifts and talents to bless many lives within his community and this industry. Driven by that mission and a keen eye for unique home design and functionality, he set out to build a company where people come before profit and integrity rules over everything. He believes that a home is more than the sticks and bricks but rather the lives of those who will live in it for years to come. From concept to completion, Forrest is hands on and committed to excellence. He cares, and it shows. Investing in people and improving the industry, one home at a time.

Why Stout Builders?

Every detail matters. From the first sketch to the final walkthrough, Stout Builders exists to deliver homes and spaces that are built with purpose — not just to code, but to a standard. We believe the construction process should be as intentional as the finished product. Clear communication, honest timelines, and a commitment to getting it right. That's what sets us apart.

Our Approach

Every project starts with a conversation. We listen to how you live, what matters to you, and where your vision is headed. From there, we coordinate design, engineering, and construction under one roof — so nothing gets lost between handoffs. One builder. One point of contact. Concept to completion.

Our Work

Based in Orlando, Stout Builders has delivered custom homes, full-scale renovations, and outdoor living projects across Central Florida. Every project in our portfolio reflects the same standard — thoughtful design, quality craftsmanship, and a process built on trust.

The Vision

Stout Builders is building toward something bigger than any single project. The long-term vision is to develop communities — neighborhoods where families grow side by side and do life together. Beyond that, Forrest is committed to using the platform God has given him to serve — whether that's locally in Orlando or wherever the need is greatest. The mission hasn't changed since day one: use construction as a tool to invest in people and build something that lasts well beyond the walls.
